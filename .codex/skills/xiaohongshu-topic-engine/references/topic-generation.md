# Topic generation

1. Extract up to three unmet audience needs, frictions, or anxieties from the material.
2. Connect a need to the content type using a conflict, contrast, or counterintuitive angle.
3. Create one save-worthy propagation anchor: memorable line, comparison image, reusable template, or user-supplied evidence.
4. Select at least two distinct content genes: emotional hook, information gap, identity label, action trigger, or counterintuitive conflict.
5. Score click potential, shareability, and executability from 1–5. `total` is their sum; sort descending and set `edge: true` below 10.

For programmatic use retain `topic`, `title`, `angle`, `anchor`, `anchor_reason`, `genes`, `hit_reason`, `score`, `edge`, and `safety`.

Avoid absolute advertising claims. In health, skincare, or medical contexts, frame content as personal experience and do not promise outcomes or give diagnoses.
